# Ideal Customer Profile

**Name (invented) and one-line sketch:** Davit, 20, 3rd-year CS student living on the 3rd floor of Block F at KIU.

**Their situation:** Takes 5 courses, lives in the dorms, does laundry once or twice a week (usually evenings or Sundays). Has to carry heavy laundry baskets down 3 flights of stairs to the basement laundry room.

**The moment the problem shows up:** Sunday evening at 21:00, when he carries his basket down 3 floors, only to find all 4 machines spinning with 40+ minutes left on the timer.

**What it costs them:** 30–40 minutes wasted per trip, carrying heavy loads up and down stairs, wet clothes smelling moldy when forgotten, and anger when someone dumps his wet clothes onto a dirty table.

**What they do about it today:** Hauls everything back up to his room, sends messages to the dorm Telegram group asking "who has machine #2?", or dumps someone else's wet laundry onto the bench.

**What they are NOT:**
- Students living in rented apartments in Kutaisi with their own private washing machine.
- Students who take their dirty laundry back home to Tbilisi every weekend.
