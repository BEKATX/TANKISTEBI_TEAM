## Beka Tkhilaishvili

AUTHOR: Beka
WHO I watched: Third-year KIU students living in Block F.
STRUGGLE I saw: Carrying laundry baskets down 3 flights of stairs only to find all 4 washing machines running with 45 minutes left, so they either dump someone’s wet clothes on the dusty table or haul everything back upstairs.
COST I can name: 30–40 minutes wasted per trip, wet clothes smelling moldy, and constant arguments in the dorm chat.

## Anastasia Khevtsuriani

AUTHOR: Anastasia
WHO I watched: KIU students staying on campus after evening lectures.
STRUGGLE I saw: After around 6:00 PM, the cafeterias on campus are closed, while many students still have classes, study sessions, or club activities. Students either stay hungry, eat snacks from vending machines, or order delivery from Kutaisi.
COST I can name: 30–60 minutes waiting for food delivery, extra delivery fees, and students losing study time or skipping meals completely.

AUTHOR: Anastasia
WHO I watched: KIU students trying to complete the required 12 internship credits.
STRUGGLE I saw: Students spend weeks searching through LinkedIn, Facebook groups, company websites, and personal contacts trying to find internships that are relevant to their field and can actually be recognized by the university for credit.
COST I can name: Hours spent searching and applying with few responses, missed internship opportunities, and the risk of not completing the required 12 credits on time.

AUTHOR: Anastasia
WHO I watched: KIU students struggling with difficult courses before exams and assignments.
STRUGGLE I saw: Students repeatedly ask in group chats if someone can explain a topic or help them with an exercise, but they often cannot quickly find a student who understands the subject and is available to help.
COST I can name: Several hours wasted searching for help or trying to learn the topic alone, assignments submitted incorrectly or late, when another student on campus could have helped them.
