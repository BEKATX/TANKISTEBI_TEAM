**Interview 02 · Resident, Block F Second Floor · (code name: Resident-F207)**

**Week:** 2 · **Asker:** AK · **Logger:** BT · **Consent:** yes; notes only, no recording  
**Real or practice:** real

**Context:** Second year student living in Block F, does laundry around once or twice a week, usually in the evening or on weekends. **Our prediction:** We are wrong if students tell us they almost always find an open machine on their first visit, or that checking machine status is not something they care about.

- "კვირას ჩავედი სარეცხის ჩასაყრელად და ყველა მანქანა დაკავებული იყო. დაახლოებით ნახევარი საათის მერე ისევ მომიწია ჩასვლა." / "I went down on Sunday to put my laundry in and every machine was occupied. I had to go down again about half an hour later."
- "ერთხელ ჩემი რეცხვა დასრულებული იყო და რომ ჩავედი, ვიღაცას უკვე ამოღებული ჰქონდა და მაგიდაზე ედო." / "Once my washing had finished, and when I came down someone had already taken it out and put it on the table." ★

- "ტაიმერს ტელეფონში ვაყენებ, მაგრამ მანქანის დრო ყოველთვის ზუსტად არ ემთხვევა." / "I set a timer on my phone, but the machine's time doesn't always match it exactly."
- "კარგი იქნებოდა ოთახიდან ვიცოდე რომელი მანქანაა თავისუფალი ან რამდენი წუთი დარჩა." / "It would be useful if I could know from my room which machine is free or how many minutes are left."

**Workaround:** Sets a phone timer and sometimes walks downstairs before the expected finishing time to check the machine. If all machines are busy, waits in the room and checks again later.

**Cost:** Usually makes 2–3 unnecessary trips downstairs and sometimes waits 20–40 minutes before a machine becomes available.

**Frequency:** Says the problem happens mostly on Sunday evenings and other busy weekday evenings.

**Prediction check:** Confirmed. The resident regularly checks machine availability manually and cares about knowing when machines become free.

**Surprise ★:** The resident did not consider removing someone else's finished laundry completely unacceptable. They said it becomes reasonable if the owner leaves it in the machine for too long. The bigger concern was having clothes placed on a dirty surface.

**Commitment:** Agreed that we can contact them again and suggested talking to students who regularly do laundry on Sunday evenings.

**Next:** In Interview 03, investigate how long students think it is acceptable to leave finished laundry inside a machine and whether they would trust machine-status information provided digitally.
