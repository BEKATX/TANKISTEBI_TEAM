# Interview Script v1

**Problem we are investigating:** how KIU dorm residents in Block F manage laundry room bottlenecks
**Our prediction:** We are wrong if students tell us they almost always find an open machine on their first visit, or that checking machine status is not something they care about.
**Language:** Georgian (English translation beside it in logs)

## Opening (2 min)
"Hi! Thanks for helping out. We're doing a project for our software product course at KIU about how daily dorm routines actually work. We are not selling anything and we don't have an app to pitch. Is it okay if my teammate takes notes? We will not use your name anywhere."

## Warm-up (2 min)
1. How long have you lived in Block F, and roughly how often do you do laundry?

## Core (12 to 15 min)
2. Walk me through the very last time you did laundry, from picking up your clothes to taking them out.
3. Tell me about a time you went down to the laundry room and things didn't go as planned.
4. When that happened, what did you do, and how much time did it take?
5. Tell me about the last time someone touched your laundry or you had to move someone else's.
6. Walk me through how you track when your washing cycle is finished.
7. Have you ever tried any tricks or asked in the group chat to avoid making an empty trip? Can you show me what you did?

## Close (2 min)
8. Who else on your floor complains about the laundry room that we should talk to?
9. Can we message you again later when we analyze the results?

## Follow-up bank
- And then what happened?
- Why was that a problem?
- When was the last time that happened?
- *Silence: count to three.*
